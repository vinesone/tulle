A 3D Gaussian Splatting scene as a Tulle source, viewed through a window camera coupled to your head. Turn on the webcam and move your head: the screen becomes a pane of glass. Things behind the focus plane follow your head, things in front of it slide the other way. No camera? The pointer stands in for your head. The scene is an ordinary layer, so it still runs through a post chain (vignette → grain).
Drop a photo on the canvas to turn it into a splat: depth is estimated in your browser (Depth Anything V2, a one-time 27 MB download), every pixel is lifted onto its ray, and you can lean around it — then download the .splat.
